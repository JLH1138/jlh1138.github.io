Integrity Coaching

For men who are ready to break free from unwanted sexual behaviors, build genuine integrity, and step into the purpose they were designed for.

Who This Is For

  • Men struggling with pornography or unwanted sexual behaviors
  • Men caught in patterns of compulsive sexual behavior
  • Men who want to rebuild trust with their spouse or family
  • Men experiencing personal, spiritual, or professional consequences from sexual patterns

Areas of Focus

  • Understanding the roots of unwanted behaviors
  • Developing practical relapse prevention strategies
  • Building emotional regulation and coping skills
  • Establishing healthy boundaries
  • Strengthening integrity, accountability, and values alignment
  • Rebuilding trust and improving relationships
  • Strengthening identity, purpose, and personal growth

The Process

1

Consultation

Confidential conversation about your situation and goals

2

Assessment

Understanding patterns, triggers, and root issues

3

Action Plan

Customized strategy with clear milestones

4

Ongoing Coaching

Regular sessions for accountability, growth, and support

My coaching approach is designed to help clients move beyond simply managing symptoms and instead develop a deeper understanding of the underlying factors that contribute to their behaviors. Together, we identify triggers, emotional patterns, beliefs, coping mechanisms, and relational dynamics that may be fueling destructive cycles.

Clients are guided through a structured process of self-awareness, accountability, emotional growth, and personal transformation.

Drawing from years of experience facilitating men's recovery groups and helping individuals pursue freedom from unwanted sexual behaviors, I provide a supportive, nonjudgmental environment where clients can pursue lasting change and personal growth.

The goal is not simply abstinence from unwanted behaviors. The goal is helping men build lives characterized by integrity, authenticity, healthy relationships, and freedom.

Ready to Begin?

The first step is a confidential consultation. No pressure, no commitment — just a conversation about where you are and where you want to go.
