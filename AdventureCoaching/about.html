About Lance Baker

The Guide Behind
the Journey

Lance Baker spent over thirty years in law enforcement — a career that taught him about pressure, integrity, consequences, and the human capacity for both failure and redemption.

The principles of recovery mirror the principles of any great expedition: honest assessment of where you are, a clear picture of where you're going, the right tools for the terrain, and someone who knows the path walking beside you.

Lance holds credentials as a Board Certified Mental Health Coach, is a Pastoral Sex Addiction Professional, has completed specialized training in disclosure facilitation, and is a Core Values Index practitioner.

Lance's approach is shaped by his faith, his experience, and his conviction that no one is beyond redemption. Lance is passionate about helping individuals and couples move from betrayal and brokenness to truth, healing, and restored connection.
