About Lance Baker

The Guide Behind
the Journey

Lance Baker spent over thirty years in law enforcement—a career that taught him about pressure, integrity, consequences, and the human capacity for both failure and redemption.

Through his own journey of restoration, Lance discovered that the principles of recovery mirror the principles of any great expedition: honest assessment of where you are, a clear picture of where you're going, the right tools for the terrain, and someone who knows the path walking beside you.

That discovery led him to professional coaching. He now holds credentials as a Board Certified Mental Health Coach, has completed specialized training in disclosure facilitation, and is a certified Core Values Index practitioner.

Disclosure &
Relational Repair

The Rite of Truth & Relational Repair Process—a guided, structured approach to honest disclosure that creates safety and lays the foundation for genuine healing.

Why Disclosure Matters

Secrets thrive in darkness. When betrayal has occurred, the path to restoration requires bringing the truth into the light—but in a way that is guided, safe, and purposeful.

Poorly handled disclosure can cause more damage than the original betrayal. That's why professional guidance is essential.

A well-facilitated disclosure gives the injured partner the information they need to make informed decisions about their future, while also creating a framework for the betraying partner to take full responsibility.

This process honors both people and prioritizes safety above all.

The Seven-Step Process

1

Initial Consultation

Confidential assessment of the situation, history, and readiness for the process.

2

Preparation & Assessment

Comprehensive preparation work with the individual preparing for disclosure.

3

Building the Disclosure Document

Creating a thorough, honest, and appropriately scoped written disclosure.

4

Partner Preparation

Ensuring the partner has appropriate support, safety planning, and expectations set.

5

The Disclosure Session

A facilitated, safe session where the disclosure is delivered with professional guidance.

6

Immediate Aftercare

Structured support for both individuals in the hours and days following disclosure.

7

Relational Repair Pathway

Transitioning into ongoing recovery work, which may include couples coaching and individual coaching.

Important Note

Disclosure services begin with a confidential consultation because each situation is unique. Direct online booking is not available for disclosure services. During the consultation, we'll assess readiness, discuss the process in detail, and determine the best path forward together.

Couples Recovery

For couples navigating the difficult terrain of betrayal recovery—rebuilding trust, restoring communication, and finding a new path forward together.

What We Address

  • Healing after betrayal and infidelity
  • Rebuilding broken trust
  • Healthy communication skills
  • Establishing and maintaining boundaries
  • Rebuilding emotional and physical intimacy
  • Creating a shared recovery plan

What Makes This Different

Couples recovery coaching is not marriage counseling. We focus on the specific dynamics of betrayal recovery and the practical steps both partners need to take.

Both partners are honored in this process. The injured partner's pain is validated, and the betraying partner's genuine effort is supported—with accountability that creates real safety.

We work from a place of hope: marriages can be restored, trust can be rebuilt, and couples can emerge stronger than before.

Core Values Index

Discover the deepest drivers of who you are. The CVI isn't a personality test—it's a lens into your core motivations that transforms how you lead, relate, and recover.

What Makes the CVI Different

Most assessments measure how you behave. The CVI measures why you behave that way—your deepest, most unchanging motivations.

It takes less than 10 minutes to complete, yet provides insights that remain accurate for a lifetime. Your core values don't change; understanding them changes everything.

Unlike personality tests that put you in a box, the CVI opens doors. It reveals your unique blend of motivations and shows you how to leverage them in every area of life.

With a 97% repeat-reliability rating, it's the most accurate assessment of its kind available.
