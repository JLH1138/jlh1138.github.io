Disclosure & Relational Repair Services
Truth. Accountability. Healing.
One of the most difficult and important steps in relational recovery following sexual betrayal is the disclosure process. As a Certified Disclosure Guide Candidate, I assist men and couples in navigating this process with honesty, structure, preparation, and care.
Many individuals attempt disclosure without adequate preparation, often causing additional confusion, trauma, and mistrust. My role is to help create a process that promotes truth, accountability, emotional safety, and relational healing.
Services may include:

-   Individual preparation for the offending partner
-   Development of a formal disclosure document
-   Clarification and organization of disclosure information
-   Timeline development and fact verification
-   Guidance regarding appropriate transparency and accountability
-   Coordination with therapists and treatment professionals when appropriate
-   Preparation for anticipated emotional responses
-   Disclosure presentation support
-   Post-disclosure support and recovery planning

For couples, the disclosure process is not simply about revealing information. It is about creating a truthful foundation from which healing, trust rebuilding, and meaningful relational repair can begin.
My Rite of Truth & Relational Repair Process provides a structured framework designed to help couples move through disclosure with greater clarity, support, and intentionality.
The goal is not merely disclosure. The goal is establishing truth as the foundation for healing.

Core Values Index (CVI) Assessment & Coaching
Discover How You Are Wired
The Core Values Index (CVI) is a powerful psychometric assessment that measures an individual's innate and unchanging core values and motivational drivers.
Unlike personality assessments that describe behavior, the CVI identifies what naturally energizes and motivates a person at their deepest level. It helps clients understand how they are uniquely wired and why they approach life, relationships, work, leadership, and decision-making the way they do.
The CVI can provide valuable insight for:

-   Personal growth and self-awareness
-   Career development and vocational alignment
-   Leadership development
-   Relationship understanding
-   Team effectiveness
-   Conflict resolution
-   Decision-making confidence
-   Recovery and personal transformation
-   Communication improvement

Through a guided CVI debrief, clients gain a clearer understanding of:

-   Their natural strengths
-   Core motivational drivers
-   Areas of potential stress and frustration
-   Communication preferences
-   Leadership tendencies
-   Relationship dynamics
-   Personal growth opportunities
-   Sources of energy and fulfillment

Understanding how a person is uniquely designed often creates powerful breakthroughs in confidence, purpose, effectiveness, and self-understanding.
Whether used for personal growth, leadership development, recovery work, or relationship enhancement, the CVI provides practical insight that clients can immediately apply to their lives.
